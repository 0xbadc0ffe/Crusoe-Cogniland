\paragraph{Gradient clamp.}
The edit \eqref{eq:clamp} is applied to the post-GRU state, so it feeds the
heads at the current step and is carried forward by the recurrence. Because
the actor is linear in $\mathbf{h}$ the gradient is available in closed form,
$\nabla_{\mathbf{h}}\log p_{\mathcal{A}} = \sum_{a\in\mathcal{A}}\tfrac{\pi(a)}{p_{\mathcal{A}}}\mathbf{W}_a
- \sum_{a}\pi(a)\mathbf{W}_a$ for actor rows $\mathbf{W}_a$. We use
$\eta=0.5$ and an iteration budget of $25$ ($80$ in the strength sweep);
the post-edit probability is recorded and met $\theta$ on every episode
reported. The commands are $\mathcal{A}=\{\textsc{build}\}$ (no bridging) and
$\mathcal{A}=\{\textsc{mine}\}$ (no tunnelling). A gated variant releases the clamp
once the agent has made no progress on the cost-to-go for a fixed number of
steps and re-arms it when progress resumes. Thresholds and windows are chosen
on balanced training maps as the strongest setting that keeps success above
$0.9$ and timeouts below $0.05$ there, and are frozen before any held-out map
is run: $\theta=5\cdot10^{-3}$ with an $8$-step window for \textsc{build},
$\theta=10^{-3}$ with a $5$-step window for \textsc{mine}
(Table~\ref{tab:clamp_all}).

\paragraph{Fixed steering vector.}
The unconditional form adds $\eta\,\mathbf{u}$ at every step for a unit
direction $\mathbf{u}$ fitted offline as the difference of state means between
steps where the tool was used and steps where it was not, or as the actor's
own gradient for the target action, $\mathbf{h}\leftarrow\mathbf{h}+\eta\,
\nabla_{\mathbf{h}}\log\pi(a\mid\mathbf{h})$.
